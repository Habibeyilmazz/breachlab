Break it, then fix it.

Quillbook is a small notes-and-invoices app with ten real security holes. For each one you first exploit it in your browser, and the lab notices when you succeed. Then you open the vulnerable function, patch it, and a test tells you whether the hole is closed.

Keep this on your own machine. BreachLab is vulnerable on purpose. It only answers on 127.0.0.1 and must never be deployed or exposed to a network.
